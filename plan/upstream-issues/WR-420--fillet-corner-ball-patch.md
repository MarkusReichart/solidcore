#### WR-420. `SmFilletSolver::CalcFilletGeom` has no fillet geometry for a round whose centre line collapses to a point
- **Severity:** Wrong result (the blend fails with `SM_ERR` 1001)
- **Area:** Blend
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletSolver.cpp` (`SmFilletSolver::CalcFilletGeom`, the analytic attempt under `CanMakeAnalytic()`): only `CalcCylinderFilletGeom` and `CalcTorusFilletGeom` are tried; the offset trace then follows.
- **What happens:** the round of the seam where two rounds as wide as itself meet (an R2 round of the edge between two R2 rounds) has every ball centred where the rounds' cylinder axes meet: both offset faces collapse onto those axes and the centre line is a single point. Neither analytic case applies (the edge is not a line or an arc about an axis), and the offset trace has no centre line to follow, so the blend returns 1001.
- **Trigger:** R2 rounds on the 4 vertical edges of a 25×25×10 box whose bottom edges are already R2: the tangent chain takes in the seam where two bottom rounds meet (SolidCraft rig case fillets/1557).
- **How found:** SolidCraft regression rig on the SMLib fork (tangency-labs/solidcore#416).
- **Confirmed:** on the fork's C++ build (tangency-labs/smlib@04bd9a5, capi regression test in `ops_kernel.cpp`, matching the closed form).
- **Fix:** a new `CalcSphereFilletGeom`, tried after the torus: for a circular section between two cylinders as wide as the round whose axes meet at the common centre of every fillet point, the fillet surface is the ball's patch, the first contact circle's arc between the edge's ends revolved about the line through the centre square to both axes onto the second contact circle (less than half a turn); the rails are its v-min and v-max iso curves.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_corner_ball_patch` (default `false`, upstream behaviour), read in `c3_try_analytic` (`crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_solver/p4.rs`); the patch is `FilletSolver::calc_sphere_fillet_geom` / `sphere_fillet_geom_parts` and `sphere_patch_parts` in `crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_solver/p2.rs`, documented in that file's Deviations. Test `issue_416_corner_ball_patch`. With the default no difftest path changes. Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#416.
