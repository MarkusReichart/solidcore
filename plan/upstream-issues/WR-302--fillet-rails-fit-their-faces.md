#### WR-302. A blend whose rails leave the faces they roll on is built across what lies beyond them
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a blend larger than its faces is built through openings or walls, or as a "valid" body) or an unrelated later failure
- **Area:** Blend
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletExecutive.cpp` (`SmFilletExecutive::CreateFilletBrep`, after the `CalcFilletGeom` loop): nothing checks that a solver's untrimmed rails stay on the faces they roll on before corners, trimming and topology insertion run.
- **What happens:** a round or chamfer whose setback exceeds the room on a face (the rail runs into a wall or an opening's rim between the filleted edge's ends) is built as if the face continued, or fails later with an unrelated status.
- **Trigger:** SolidCraft rig fillets/1080 (a cube 20 with three 10 x 10 through openings 5 mm in) rounded or chamfered 6 to 9 on its top front edge; slab 20 x 20 x 4 at 5 to 8; case 3435 at 4.9 to 6.8; a 10 x 20 x 30 box's top edge x = 10, z = 30 rounded by a variable law from 2 to 20 or 30 (tangency-labs/solidcore#841, comment of 2026-10-25).
- **How found:** SolidCraft regression rig on the SMLib fork.
- **Confirmed:** on the fork's C++ build: tangency-labs/smlib f0edce8ce (`SmFilletSolver::RailsFitTheirFaces`, `SM_FILERR_NO_ROOM`), amended in #841 (`LeavesPastAnEnd`: a rail may leave its face only past an end of the filleted edge, across the face's side edge there or through one of its vertices, or on or beyond the cross section at an end) and #1024 (a run of off-face intervals split at a boundary vertex leaves the face once; rig case fillets/4365 then builds with its analytic volume).
- **Fix:** after `CalcFilletGeom`, classify each default geometry's untrimmed rail against its original face (`SmFace::CurveOnClassify`); a rail nowhere on its face, or leaving it beside the filleted edge across an edge that is not a tangent rollover (`sm_TestManifoldTangentRollover`), refuses the blend with `SM_FILERR_NO_ROOM`.
- **Rust port:** `FilletSolver::rails_fit_their_faces` / `rails_fit_their_faces_with_context` (with `off_face_runs` and `leaves_past_an_end`) in `crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_solver/p5.rs`; the executive calls it after the solver geometry only with the opt-in `Kernel::fillet_refuse_rails_leaving_faces` (default `false`, upstream behaviour) and fails with `FilletErrorType::NoRoom`. Test `issue_1024_off_face_run_through_a_vertex_is_judged_once`. Default off, so no difftest case changes. Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#1024; the rail check of tangency-labs/solidcore#841 (that issue's Nx1 end-rib deletion stays blocked on the pseudo-B-rep `SmBrep::DeleteEdge`, `UNPORTED(topology.brep_p4)`).
