#### WR-301. `SmFilletSolver::TestRollOver` takes a rail running along its face's rim as leaving the face
- **Severity:** Blend failure (`SM_ERR`, `SM_FILERR_EDGE_PROBLEM` "Fillet Solver Error: Unable to Process Rollover")
- **Area:** Blend
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletSolver.cpp:5747` (`SmFilletSolver::TestRollOver`, `SmFace::CurveOnClassify` of each rail) and `:7161` (`ProcessRollOver`), failing in `sm_FindRolloverSideEdge` (`:6146` / `:6302`, "Unknown case").
- **What happens:** `CurveOnClassify` classifies a rail against its original face: `SM_PC_UNKNOWN` outside, `SM_PC_FACE` inside, `SM_PC_EDGE` / `SM_PC_VERTEX` on the boundary. `ProcessRollOver` takes every interior interval boundary as the rail crossing a side edge into another face. A rail that touches its face's rim at a vertex or runs along a piece of a boundary edge gives FACE / EDGE / FACE intervals ending on vertices; `sm_FindRolloverSideEdge` requires an interior interval to end on an `SM_PC_EDGE` and returns `SM_ERR`. The rail never leaves its face.
- **Trigger:** a cube 20 with three orthogonal 10 x 10 through openings 5 mm in (SolidCraft rig case fillets/1080), its top front edge (y = 0, z = 20) chamfered or rounded at exactly 5, so the setback reaches the openings' rims. Sizes 4 and 6 to 9 build.
- **How found:** SolidCraft regression rig on the SMLib fork (tangency-labs/solidcore#485).
- **Confirmed:** no (read at c9c5979 `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletSolver.cpp:5714` (`TestRollOver`), `:7161` (`ProcessRollOver`) and `:6146` / `:6302` (`sm_FindRolloverSideEdge`); reproduced on the SolidCraft fork build, tangency-labs/smlib f6ed05ab6: chamfer 5 gives 3750 and round 5 gives 3892.6991, the closed forms 4000 - 12.5 x 20 and 4000 - (1 - pi/4) x 25 x 20; the fork's other sweep sizes are unchanged).
- **Fix:** before the short-interval squeeze, neighbouring intervals whose mids are on the face or on its boundary are joined (`SmCurveClassification::ConnectIntervals(jj, SM_IP_START, FALSE)`, from the last pair down, taking the later mid where the earlier one is not `SM_PC_FACE`), so only the parts of a rail outside its face roll over.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fillet_rail_rim_on_face` (default `false`, upstream behaviour), read by `finish_classification` / `join_on_face_intervals` in `crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_solver/p5.rs`; documented in that file's Deviations. Test `issue_485_rail_along_rim_stays_on_face`. Default off, so `blend.fillet_solver_p5` and the blend units keep comparing upstream's classification. Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#485.
