#### WR-401. `SmPrimitiveCreation::CreateRotationalSweep` makes analytic faces only for a one-curve profile
- **Severity:** Wrong result (geometry type): no cylinder, cone, sphere or torus faces on a revolved multi-curve profile
- **Area:** Primitive creation / sweeps
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmPrimitiveCreation.cpp`, `CreateRotationalSweep`: only a one-curve profile goes to `sm_CreateAnalyticRotationalSweep`; every curve of a longer profile becomes an `SmSurfOfRevolution` (a whole turn, `USE_NEW_ANALYTICS`) or, for a partial turn, a B-spline surface of revolution through `SmRotationalSweepGeometry::EdgeSweepHigher`. `SmBSplineSurface::CopyAndAddAnalytics` does not recover the analytic kind afterwards: its `SmCylinder`/`SmTorus` recognition is commented out, and its `SmCone`/`SmSphere` copies are parameterised differently from the B-spline the face's trims are made on.
- **What happens:** a drilled hole with a drill point, a torus from a revolved circle profile or a dome built by revolving a multi-curve profile has no analytic faces.
- **Trigger:** `SmApiCreateRotationalSweep` of any profile of two or more curves (e.g. a rectangle off the axis: two surfaces of revolution where two cylinders are expected).
- **How found:** reported by jyviko (tangency-labs/solidcore#545); fixed in tangency-labs/smlib 0b143f7 (branch port-revolve-analytic).
- **Confirmed:** by reading `CreateRotationalSweep`; the port reproduces it.
- **Fix:** sweep each profile curve to its analytic surface, bounded to the curve and the turn: a line parallel to the axis to a cylinder, another line to a cone, an arc centred on the axis to a sphere, an arc off the axis in a plane through it to a torus.
- **Rust port:** `crates/solidcore/src/sweep/rotational_sweep_geometry.rs` (`revolved_analytic_surface`: the surface over the turn's angle, u the angle counter-clockwise about the axis from the curve's half-plane), used by `PrimitiveCreation::create_rotational_sweep` (`crates/solidcore/src/primitive/primitive_creation/p3/sweeps.rs`) for each curve of a full turn and by `RotationalSweepGeometry::edge_sweep_higher` for a partial turn in place of `sm_CreateRotSrf` (where the analytic surface's v runs against the edge curve, the loop is reversed as upstream does for its plane, so the face keeps the B-spline surface's side). Opt-in `Kernel::fixes.revolve_analytic_profile_faces` (off by default: upstream's surfaces of revolution, so the sweep difftest units keep matching the C++). Lines perpendicular to the axis keep upstream's planar annulus. Not covered: lines crossing the axis, arcs through a pole and tubes touching the axis (those keep the surface of revolution). Tests: `issue_545_revolved_profile_lines_are_cylinders` and `issue_545_partial_turn_lines_are_cylinders` in `sweeps.rs`.
- Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#545.
