#### WR-403. A full-turn `CreateRotationalSweep` of a profile with nested loops makes the inner shell a second solid
- **Severity:** Wrong result (two solids instead of a hollow one; volume summed)
- **Area:** Primitive creation / sweeps
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmPrimitiveCreation.cpp`, `SmPrimitiveCreation::CreateRotationalSweep` (curve-list overload, the `dRotAngleDeg >= 359.99999` path): every profile curve's face is made in the infinite region and `StitchAndOrient` stitches them. `TestClosureSplitRegion` gives each closed shell its own solid region and nests only the *other* shells of the region it splits, so a shell closed inside an existing solid stays in the infinite region; `OrientTrimmedSurfaces` then skips its faces (their upward face use is in the infinite region) and it stays an outward-facing solid.
- **What happens:** a half annulus (line (18.5,0)-(40,0), arc r40, line (-40,0)-(-18.5,0), arc r18.5) revolved 360 degrees about the x axis gives two balls, V = 294604.5, where a hollow ball is 241560.7. A 0.999 turn and sphere minus sphere are right.
- **Trigger:** `SmApiCreateRotationalSweep` of a full turn whose profile revolves into nested closed shells.
- **How found:** reported by jyviko (tangency-labs/solidcore#478); fixed in the SolidCraft fork (branch r-booleans, 0993713).
- **Confirmed:** by reading `CreateRotationalSweep`, `TestClosureSplitRegion` and `OrientTrimmedSurfaces`; not compared on the C++ reference here.
- **Fix:** after the stitch, for each solid region with one shell whose mate is in the infinite region, fire a ray (as `TestClosureSplitRegion`'s shell nesting does) from a point of its outside shell at every other such solid's inside shell. A solid inside exactly one other (which is inside none) has its outside shell moved into that solid's region, its region marked void, and its faces swapped to point into the cavity. Deeper nesting is left as built.
- **Rust port:** `crates/solidcore/src/topology/brep/p6.rs` (`nest_closed_shells_as_cavities`), called from `PrimitiveCreation::create_rotational_sweep` (`crates/solidcore/src/primitive/primitive_creation/p3/sweeps.rs`) when the opt-in `Kernel::fixes.revolve_nested_shells_as_cavities` is set (off by default: upstream's separate solids, so the sweep difftest units keep matching the C++). Test: `issue_478_full_turn_nested_shell_is_a_cavity` in `sweeps.rs`.
- Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#478.
